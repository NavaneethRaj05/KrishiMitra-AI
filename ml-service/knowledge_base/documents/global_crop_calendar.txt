Global Crop Calendar and Agricultural Advisory

CLIMATE PROFILE CATEGORIES
Tropical Humid: Annual rainfall 1500-4000mm, Red laterite/alluvial soils, Year-round growing, Temperature 22-35°C.
Tropical Semi-Arid: Annual rainfall 500-1000mm, Black and red loamy soils, Distinct kharif/rabi seasons, Temperature 20-40°C.
Temperate: Annual rainfall 400-900mm, Loam to clay soils, Single main growing season, Temperature 5-25°C.
Mediterranean: Annual rainfall 300-700mm, Clay loam, Winter growing season, Summer drought, Temperature 10-30°C.

KHARIF SEASON (June – November, Tropical South Asia / Sub-Saharan Africa)

Ragi (Finger Millet / Eleusine coracana):
- Sowing: June 15 – July 15 (monsoon onset)
- Recommended varieties: GPU-28, MR-6, Indaf-5, VL-149
- Spacing: 22.5 x 10 cm
- Seed rate: 10 kg/ha (transplanted), 5 kg/ha (direct seeded)
- Fertilizer: N:P:K = 100:50:50 kg/ha
- Irrigation: Rainfed in normal years; supplemental irrigation at panicle initiation
- Yield: 3-4 tonnes/ha (improved varieties)
- Key pests: Blast (Pyricularia grisea) — spray Tricyclazole 75% WP 0.6g/L

Rice (Paddy / Oryza sativa):
- Sowing: June 20 – July 20 (monsoon zones); varies by region
- Transplanting: 20-25 days old seedlings
- Recommended varieties: Jyothi, Surekha, IR-64, BPT-5204, IRRI-6, BRRI dhan28 (vary by region)
- Spacing: 20 x 15 cm
- Fertilizer: N:P:K = 100:50:50 kg/ha; apply in 3 splits
- Water management: Maintain 5cm standing water during vegetative phase
- Yield: 5-6 tonnes/ha

Maize (Zea mays):
- Sowing: June 15 – July 10 (kharif); February-March (spring)
- Recommended varieties: KHDH-9, NK-6240, Pioneer 3396, DKC varieties
- Spacing: 60 x 20 cm
- Fertilizer: N:P:K = 150:75:75 kg/ha
- Irrigation: 6-7 irrigations; critical stages: knee-high, tasseling, grain filling
- Yield: 7-8 tonnes/ha (hybrids)

Sorghum (Jowar / Sorghum bicolor):
- Sowing: June-July (kharif)
- Varieties: CSH-16, SPV-669, Maldandi (South Asia); IS-3541, Sorghum hybrids (Africa, Americas)
- Spacing: 45 x 15 cm
- Fertilizer: N:60, P:30, K:30 kg/ha
- Yield: 3-4 t/ha (hybrids)

RABI SEASON (November – March, South Asia / Winter crops globally)

Tomato:
- Transplanting: October 15 – November 30 (tropical rabi); March-May (temperate spring)
- Best varieties: Arka Rakshak, COTH-3, Naveen; local hybrids adapted to region
- Spacing: 60 x 45 cm (stakes required for indeterminate varieties)
- Temperature requirement: 15–25°C (night) is ideal for fruit set
- Disease management: Spray copper oxychloride 3g/L preventively

Potato (Solanum tuberosum):
- Planting: September – November (tropical highlands); March-April (temperate zones)
- Varieties: Kufri Jyoti, Kufri Sindhuri (South Asia); Atlantic, Russet Burbank (Americas, Europe)
- Seed rate: 2000-2500 kg/ha (cut tubers, 30-40g each)
- Spacing: 60 x 20 cm
- Fertilizer: N:P:K = 120:80:100 kg/ha
- Late blight is major disease — spray Metalaxyl + Mancozeb every 7-10 days

Wheat (Triticum aestivum):
- Sowing: October-November (South Asia); October-December (temperate zones)
- Varieties: HD-2967, PBW-343, GW-496 (South Asia); Winter Red, SRW varieties (global)
- Seed rate: 100-125 kg/ha
- Spacing: 22.5 cm row-to-row
- Fertilizer: N:P:K = 120:60:40 kg/ha
- Irrigation: Crown root initiation, tillering, jointing, flowering, grain fill (5 critical stages)
- Yield: 4-5 t/ha irrigated, 2-3 t/ha rainfed

PERENNIAL CROPS

Coconut (Cocos nucifera):
- Planting: June-July or September-October
- Varieties: Tall (West Coast Tall, East Coast Tall), Hybrid (COD x WCT), MATAG
- Spacing: 7.5 x 7.5 m (triangular)
- Fertilizer: 1 kg N, 0.5 kg P, 2 kg K per palm per year in 3 splits
- Irrigation: 40-45 litres/palm/day in summer months

Banana (Musa spp.):
- Varieties: Grand Naine, Robusta, Nendran, Cavendish
- Planting: Year-round in tropical zones
- Spacing: 1.8 x 1.5 m (tissue culture plants)
- Fertilizer: 200 g N + 60 g P + 300 g K per plant per year
- Irrigation: Drip preferred, 10-15 L/plant/day
- Yield: 25-40 t/ha

SOIL HEALTH MANAGEMENT
- Green manure crops: Sunhemp, Dhaincha, Crotalaria — incorporate at 45 days
- Vermicompost application: 2-4 tonnes/ha improves soil organic matter
- Lime application for acidic soils (pH < 5.5): 2-4 tonnes/ha
- Soil testing: Recommended every 3 years at local soil testing lab or KVK/extension office

INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT (IPM)
- Pheromone traps for fall armyworm monitoring in maize: 5/ha
- Trichogramma egg parasitoid cards: 1 lakh/ha for lepidopteran pests
- Yellow sticky traps for thrips and whitefly: 25/ha
- Neem-based products: NSKE 5% spray for sucking pests
- Trichoderma viride: 2.5 kg/ha (soil application) for soil-borne pathogens

ADVISORY NOTE
Recommendations should be adapted to local climate, soil type, and variety availability.
Contact your nearest agricultural extension office, KVK, or Ministry of Agriculture for region-specific guidance.
