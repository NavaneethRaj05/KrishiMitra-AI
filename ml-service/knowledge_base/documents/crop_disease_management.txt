TOMATO DISEASES

1. Early Blight (Alternaria solani)
Symptoms: Small brown spots with concentric rings on lower leaves, surrounded by yellow halo. Spots enlarge and merge, causing leaf drop. Spreads upward.
Cause: Fungal pathogen Alternaria solani, favored by warm (24-29°C) humid conditions.
Chemical Control: Mancozeb 75% WP at 2.5 g/L water, or Chlorothalonil 75% WP at 2g/L. Spray every 7-10 days.
Organic Control: Neem oil 1500 PPM at 5ml/L, or Trichoderma viride 5g/L. Apply in evening.
Prevention: Use certified disease-free seeds, practice crop rotation (avoid solanaceous crops for 2 years), maintain plant spacing, avoid overhead irrigation.

2. Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans)
Symptoms: Water-soaked dark brown lesions on leaves and stems with white fungal growth on undersides. Fruits develop firm brown rot. Spreads very rapidly in cool humid weather.
Cause: Oomycete Phytophthora infestans. Favored by cool (10-25°C) humid conditions.
Chemical Control: Metalaxyl + Mancozeb 72% WP at 2.5g/L, or Cymoxanil + Mancozeb. Spray immediately at first appearance.
Organic Control: Copper-based fungicide (Copper hydroxide) at 3g/L. Bordeaux mixture 1%.
Prevention: Avoid late evening irrigation, remove infected plant debris, use resistant varieties (Arka Rakshak, PKM-1).

3. Fusarium Wilt (Fusarium oxysporum f.sp. lycopersici)
Symptoms: Yellowing of lower leaves, wilting during hot part of day, eventual plant collapse. Brown discoloration of vascular tissue visible when stem is cut.
Control: Soil drenching with Carbendazim 50% WP at 1g/L. Trichoderma viride 4g/kg seed treatment for prevention.
Prevention: Grafting tomato on wilt-resistant rootstocks, crop rotation, soil solarization.

4. Leaf Mosaic Virus (Tobacco Mosaic Virus - TMV)
Symptoms: Mosaic pattern of light and dark green patches on leaves, leaf distortion, stunted growth.
Control: No direct chemical cure. Control aphid vectors with Imidacloprid 17.8 SL at 0.3 ml/L. Rogue out infected plants.
Prevention: Use TMV-resistant varieties. Control insects early. Wash hands with soap after handling infected plants.

RICE DISEASES

1. Rice Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae)
Symptoms: Diamond-shaped lesions with gray centers and brown borders on leaves. Neck rot causes panicle to break and fall. Most devastating rice disease worldwide.
Chemical Control: Tricyclazole 75% WP at 0.6g/L or Isoprothiolane 40% EC at 1.5ml/L. Apply at boot leaf stage.
Organic Control: Silicon-based fertilizers increase blast resistance. Pseudomonas fluorescens 5g/L spray.
Prevention: Balanced fertilization (avoid excess nitrogen), use blast-resistant varieties (MTU 7029, Jyothi, IR 64), maintain optimum plant spacing.

2. Brown Spot (Helminthosporium oryzae)
Symptoms: Oval or circular brown spots on leaves and glumes. Spots have yellow halo. Indicates nutrient stress (particularly potassium deficiency).
Control: Mancozeb 75% WP at 2g/L. Correct potassium deficiency with MOP 25 kg/ha.
Prevention: Balanced fertilization, use certified seeds, treat seeds with Carboxin 37.5% + Thiram 37.5% at 3g/kg.

3. Bacterial Leaf Blight (Xanthomonas oryzae)
Symptoms: Water-soaked yellow stripes on leaf margins progressing to straw-yellow or white. Spreads downward. Kresek phase causes seedling death.
Control: No effective curative fungicide. Spray Copper Oxychloride 50% WP at 3g/L. Avoid excess nitrogen.
Prevention: Use resistant varieties, treat seeds with bleaching powder 25g/10L, avoid transplanting from infected nurseries.

WHEAT DISEASES

1. Yellow/Stripe Rust (Puccinia striiformis)
Symptoms: Yellow-orange pustules arranged in stripes along leaf veins. More common at high altitudes and cooler temperatures.
Chemical Control: Propiconazole 25% EC at 0.1% spray, or Tebuconazole 25.9% EC. Apply at first appearance.
Prevention: Use resistant varieties (K 307, PBW 343, HD 2781), timely sowing to avoid high disease pressure period.

2. Loose Smut (Ustilago tritici)
Symptoms: Grains replaced by black powdery mass of smut spores. Entire ear head affected.
Control: Seed treatment with Vitavax Power (Carboxin + Thiram) 3g/kg seed before sowing.
Prevention: Use certified disease-free seed every season.

COTTON DISEASES

1. Fusarium/Verticillium Wilt
Symptoms: Yellowing and browning of leaves starting from margins, wilting, death of plants. Brown vascular discoloration.
Control: Seed treatment with Carbendazim 2g/kg. Soil drench with Carbendazim + Thiram.
Prevention: Crop rotation with non-host crops, use wilt-resistant Bt cotton hybrids.

2. Leaf Curl Virus (Cotton Leaf Curl Disease - CLCuD)
Symptoms: Upward or downward curling of leaves, thickening of veins, development of leaf-like structures (enations) on underside.
Control: Control whitefly vector with Imidacloprid 17.8 SL at 0.5 ml/L or Thiamethoxam 25 WG at 0.3g/L.
Prevention: Use CLCuD-tolerant varieties, destroy infected plants, remove weed hosts.

GENERAL IPM (INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT) PRINCIPLES
1. Monitor fields regularly (twice a week)
2. Record pest populations and keep spray diary
3. Use Economic Threshold Levels (ETL) to decide when to spray
4. Prefer biological control agents (Trichoderma, Pseudomonas, Beauveria bassiana)
5. Use resistant varieties as first defense
6. Alternate fungicide/insecticide groups to prevent resistance
7. Read and follow label instructions carefully
8. Wear protective equipment when spraying
9. Do not spray within 7 days of harvest (pre-harvest interval)
