Rice Disease Management Guide — ICAR

RICE BLAST (Pyricularia oryzae / Magnaporthe oryzae)

The most devastating rice disease worldwide. Can cause 10-100% yield loss.

Symptoms:
- Leaf blast: Diamond-shaped lesions with grey center and brown margins
- Neck blast: Infected panicle neck turns brown, causing panicle to fall (broken neck)
- Node blast: Blackening of nodes, plant breaks at infected node

Favourable conditions:
- Temperature: 24–28°C
- High humidity (>90%)
- Excessive nitrogen fertilization
- Night temperature below 20°C combined with high humidity

Management:
Chemical control:
- Tricyclazole 75% WP: 0.6g per liter water — spray at first sign
- Propiconazole 25% EC: 1ml per liter water
- Carbendazim 50% WP: 1g per liter water
- Preventive spray interval: every 10-12 days during susceptible stage

Cultural control:
- Use resistant varieties: Jyothi, IR-64, BPT-5204
- Balanced nitrogen application — split into 3 doses
- Avoid late evening irrigation that keeps foliage wet overnight
- Remove and destroy infected plant debris after harvest

BROWN SPOT (Helminthosporium oryzae)

Symptoms: Oval to circular brown spots on leaves, 1-2mm diameter with yellow halo

Management:
- Spray Mancozeb 75% WP (2g/L) or Hexaconazole 5% EC (1ml/L)
- Treat seeds with Carbendazim 2g/kg before sowing

BACTERIAL LEAF BLIGHT (Xanthomonas oryzae)

Symptoms: Water-soaked leaf margins turning yellow then white. 
Kresek phase: Seedling wilting and death.

Management:
- No effective chemical control for bacterial diseases
- Use resistant varieties
- Avoid high nitrogen doses
- Drain fields when disease appears

SHEATH BLIGHT (Rhizoctonia solani)

Symptoms: Oval lesions on leaf sheath at waterline, spreading upwards

Management:
- Validamycin 3% L: 2ml/L water
- Hexaconazole 5% SC: 2ml/L water
- Avoid excessive N fertilizer
- Reduce plant density

STEM BORER (Chilo suppressalis, Scirpophaga incertulas)

Symptoms: Dead heart in vegetative stage, white ear in reproductive stage

Management:
- Pheromone traps: 5/ha for monitoring
- Release Trichogramma japonicum: 1 lakh/ha, 3 times at 10-day intervals
- Chemical: Chlorpyriphos 20% EC 2.5ml/L, spray when egg mass hatching begins
- Collection and destruction of egg masses

INTEGRATED DISEASE MANAGEMENT CALENDAR:
- At sowing: Seed treatment with Carbendazim + Thiram
- 20-25 DAT: First preventive spray (Mancozeb) if blast-prone area
- 35-40 DAT: Apply Trichoderma viride granules for soil diseases
- Panicle initiation: Critical stage — spray for blast and sheath blight
- Heading: Spray fungicide if neck blast risk is high
