Government Agricultural Schemes for Farmers in India

PRADHAN MANTRI FASAL BIMA YOJANA (PMFBY)
PMFBY is India's flagship crop insurance scheme providing financial support to farmers suffering crop loss or damage due to unforeseen events like natural calamities, pests and diseases. Premium rates are very low: 2% for Kharif crops, 1.5% for Rabi crops, and 5% for annual commercial and horticultural crops. The government pays the remaining premium. Farmers can enroll through Common Service Centres, banks, or the PMFBY portal (pmfby.gov.in). The scheme covers all food crops, oilseeds, and annual commercial crops.

PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
Under PM-KISAN, eligible farmer families receive Rs. 6,000 per year in three equal installments of Rs. 2,000 directly into their bank accounts. Eligibility: All land-holding farmer families with cultivable land. Farmers must register on the PM-KISAN portal (pmkisan.gov.in) with Aadhaar, bank account, and land records. Exclusions: Income tax payers, institutional landholders, government employees.

KISAN CREDIT CARD (KCC) SCHEME
KCC provides farmers with timely and adequate credit for agricultural operations, post-harvest expenses, and maintenance. Credit limit based on farm size and crop pattern. Interest rate: 7% per annum (4% effective with government subsidy for timely repayment). Available through all cooperative banks, regional rural banks, and commercial banks. KCC can be used as ATM card for cash withdrawal.

SOIL HEALTH CARD SCHEME
The government provides Soil Health Cards to farmers every two years showing the nutrient status of their soil with recommendations on fertilizer dosages. Farmers can get soil tested at designated labs. The card shows levels of 12 nutrients: N, P, K, pH, Sulphur, Zinc, Boron, Iron, Manganese, Copper, and Organic Carbon.

NATIONAL AGRICULTURE MARKET (e-NAM)
e-NAM is a pan-India electronic trading portal linking agricultural produce market committees (APMCs). Farmers can sell their produce online to buyers across India for better price discovery. Accessible via enam.gov.in or the eNAM mobile app.

PARAMPARAGAT KRISHI VIKAS YOJANA (PKVY)
PKVY promotes organic farming through a cluster-based approach. Financial assistance of Rs. 50,000 per hectare over 3 years. Cluster size: 50 farmers for 50 acres. Support includes organic inputs, certification, and marketing.

REGIONAL / STATE-LEVEL SCHEMES
Many countries and states have their own agricultural support schemes. Farmers should contact:
- Local agriculture department or extension office
- Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) — India's district-level farm science centers
- Ministry of Agriculture portal for national schemes

INDIA — EXAMPLES OF STATE SCHEMES
Karnataka: Raitha Siri (Rs. 25,000/family), Krishibhagya (farm ponds + drip), Bhoomi (digital land records at bhoomi.karnataka.gov.in)
Punjab: Mukhyamantri Pani Bachao Paise Kamao (water savings incentive), Kisan Karj Mafi (loan waiver)
Maharashtra: Gopinath Munde Farmer Accident Insurance Scheme, Shivkar crop support
Andhra Pradesh: YSR Rythu Bharosa (Rs. 13,500/acre/year), free crop insurance
Telangana: Rythu Bandhu (Rs. 5,000/acre per season), Rythu Bima insurance scheme

AFRICA — EXAMPLES
Kenya: Agricultural Insurance, e-voucher subsidy programs (AGRA supported)
Nigeria: Anchor Borrowers Programme, Growth Enhancement Support Scheme
Ghana: Planting for Food and Jobs (subsidized inputs for smallholders)
Ethiopia: Agricultural Commercialization Clusters, e-voucher fertilizer subsidy

SOUTHEAST ASIA — EXAMPLES
Bangladesh: SAAO and DAE extension services, fertilizer subsidy programs
Philippines: RCEF (Rice Competitiveness Enhancement Fund), crop insurance (PCIC)
Indonesia: Subsidi Pupuk (fertilizer subsidy), KUR (credit scheme for farmers)
Vietnam: Mekong Delta crop insurance, VND 100,000 billion credit for agriculture

HOW TO ACCESS SCHEMES
1. Visit nearest Agriculture Department / Extension Office
2. Carry identity documents, land records, bank account details
3. Check national portals: pmkisan.gov.in, pmfby.gov.in, enam.gov.in (India)
4. Contact KVK / agricultural university helplines for block-level guidance
5. Call toll-free Kisan Call Centre: 1800-180-1551 (India)
6. Check FAO country offices for international smallholder support programs
