ICAR COMPREHENSIVE PADDY (RICE) DISEASE AND CULTIVATION GUIDE

SOURCE: Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR-CRRI), Cuttack
AUTHORITY: ICAR-National Rice Research Institute, Directorate of Rice Research, Hyderabad

=============================================================
RICE BLAST DISEASE (Magnaporthe oryzae)
=============================================================

OVERVIEW: Most destructive disease of rice worldwide. Can cause 30-100% yield loss.

TYPES AND SYMPTOMS:
Leaf Blast:
- Diamond-shaped grey-white lesions with brown borders
- Lesions have narrow pointed ends (eye-shaped / spindle-shaped)
- Center of lesion is grey-white; margin is dark brown to reddish-brown
- Severe infection: entire leaf dies; plants look burnt

Neck/Panicle Blast:
- Brown or black lesion at neck of panicle (just below grain head)
- Affected panicle breaks at neck and hangs down (neck rot)
- Grain filling stops — panicle turns white and sterile
- Most damaging stage: occurs at flowering

Node Blast:
- Dark brown-black lesion at stem nodes
- Infected node breaks; plant falls over

FAVORABLE CONDITIONS:
- Temperature: 20-28°C (optimal 25°C)
- Relative Humidity: above 90%, especially overnight dew
- Excess nitrogen application (increases soft, succulent tissue)
- Cool cloudy weather for 3+ consecutive days

CHEMICAL CONTROL (per litre water):
- Tricyclazole 75% WP: 0.6 g/litre (systemic, most effective)
- Isoprothiolane 40% EC: 1.5 ml/litre (systemic, also prevents sheath blight)
- Tebuconazole 25.9% EC: 1.0 ml/litre
- Propiconazole 25% EC: 1.0 ml/litre (good for leaf blast)
- Carbendazim 50% WP: 1.0 g/litre (preventive)

SPRAY TIMING: At tillering stage AND at panicle initiation (50% heading)
SPRAY INTERVAL: 10-14 days during disease season

BIOLOGICAL CONTROL:
- Pseudomonas fluorescens (Pf-1): 5 g/litre foliar spray (3-4 applications)
- Seed treatment with Pseudomonas fluorescens 10 g/kg seed
- Trichoderma viride: 4 g/kg seed + 2.5 kg/acre soil application

MANAGEMENT:
- Drain field water for 3-4 days to reduce canopy humidity
- Reduce nitrogen top dressing during blast-favorable weather
- Avoid planting in low-lying, shaded, foggy areas
- BLAST-RESISTANT VARIETIES: BPT 5204 (Samba Mahsuri), Jyothi, IR-64, Swarna-Sub1, MTU-7029

=============================================================
RICE BROWN SPOT (Cochliobolus miyabeanus)
=============================================================

SYMPTOMS:
- Round to oval dark brown lesions with yellow halo
- Lesions have white-grey center in severe cases
- Appears on leaves, leaf sheaths, and glumes
- Common in nutrient-deficient fields (especially potassium)
- Associated with drought stress and silicon deficiency

CHEMICAL CONTROL:
- Mancozeb 75% WP: 2.5 g/litre
- Zineb 75% WP: 2.5 g/litre
- Edifenphos 50% EC: 1.0 ml/litre

MANAGEMENT:
- Potassium fertilization critical — apply MOP at 20 kg K2O/acre
- Silicon application: 300-400 kg/acre slag (if available)
- Drain and irrigate alternately to reduce stress
- Treat seeds with Thiram or Captan 3 g/kg before sowing

=============================================================
SHEATH BLIGHT (Rhizoctonia solani)
=============================================================

SYMPTOMS:
- Oval/elliptical water-soaked lesions on leaf sheaths
- Lesion center: light green to white, bordered by dark brown margin
- Lesions coalesce, sheaths and leaves die
- Spreads up to flag leaf during epidemic; sclerotia (small brown balls) on affected tissue

FAVORABLE CONDITIONS: Dense planting, high humidity, high nitrogen, continuous flooding.

CHEMICAL CONTROL:
- Hexaconazole 5% EC: 2.0 ml/litre (most commonly recommended)
- Propiconazole 25% EC: 1.0 ml/litre
- Validamycin 3% L: 2.5 ml/litre (specific to sheath blight)
- Isoprothiolane 40% EC: 1.5 ml/litre

MANAGEMENT:
- Reduce plant density — wider spacing (20x15cm)
- Split nitrogen application — avoid excess urea
- Drain field periodically
- Biocontrol: Pseudomonas fluorescens 5 g/litre, 2-3 sprays

=============================================================
BACTERIAL BLIGHT (Xanthomonas oryzae pv. oryzae)
=============================================================

SYMPTOMS:
- Water-soaked wave-like margins along leaf edges
- Yellowing and drying from leaf tips/margins (leaf scorch)
- Exudate (bacterial ooze) dries as droplets
- Kresek symptom: seedlings wilt and die at transplanting stage (severe form)
- Yellow droplets on fresh cut ends

FAVORABLE CONDITIONS: High humidity, strong winds, heavy rain, flood irrigation.

MANAGEMENT:
- Use RESISTANT VARIETIES: IR-64, Jaya, Pusa Basmati-1, Swarna-Sub1
- Spray Copper oxychloride 50% WP: 3 g/litre OR Streptocycline 0.1g + Copper oxychloride 3g/litre
- Avoid flood irrigation; do not work in wet fields
- Balanced fertilization — avoid excess nitrogen

=============================================================
PADDY STEM BORER (Scirpophaga incertulas)
=============================================================

SYMPTOMS:
- Dead heart: central tiller wilts and dries up at tillering stage
- White ear: panicle emerges empty/sterile
- Holes in stems, frass (excreta) inside stem

CHEMICAL CONTROL:
- Chlorpyrifos 20% EC: 2.5 ml/litre
- Monocrotophos 36% SL: 1.5 ml/litre (restricted use)
- Cartap hydrochloride 50% SP: 1.5 g/litre
- Fipronil 0.3% GR: 8 kg/acre (granule application to water)

BIOLOGICAL CONTROL:
- Trichogramma japonicum: 1 lakh eggs/acre (egg parasitoid, 2-3 releases at 10-day intervals)
- Scirpophaga NPV + Bt combination

=============================================================
PADDY FERTILIZER SCHEDULE (per acre)
=============================================================

TOTAL NPK REQUIREMENT:
- Nitrogen (N): 40-50 kg
- Phosphorus (P): 24-30 kg
- Potassium (K): 16-20 kg

APPLICATION:
- Basal (before transplanting): DAP 50 kg + MOP 13 kg + Urea 22 kg
- 1st Top Dress (30 days after transplanting / active tillering): Urea 33 kg
- 2nd Top Dress (55-60 days after transplanting / panicle initiation): Urea 33 kg + MOP 13 kg

ZINC MANAGEMENT (Very Important in Rice):
- Zinc deficiency very common in paddy — symptoms: stunted seedlings, brown rust-like spots
- Apply Zinc Sulfate (ZnSO4): 10 kg/acre as basal
- Foliar spray: 0.5% ZnSO4 solution at 30 and 50 days after transplanting
- Seedling dipping in 2% ZnO before transplanting (in zinc-deficient soils)

SILICON APPLICATION:
- 300-400 kg calcium silicate per acre (strengthens cell wall, reduces blast)

=============================================================
RICE CROP CALENDAR — INDIA
=============================================================

KHARIF (Main season): June-July planting; Harvest October-November
RABI (Short duration): November-December; Harvest February-March (coastal areas)

RECOMMENDED VARIETIES:
- Karnataka: BPT-5204 (Samba Mahsuri), Jyothi, MTU-1010, Kaveri (IR-36)
- Andhra Pradesh/Telangana: MTU-7029, Tellahamsa, BPT-5204
- Tamil Nadu: CO-51, ADT-45, CR Dhan 310
- Kerala: Uma, Jyothi, Ptb-33
- Punjab/Haryana (Basmati): Pusa Basmati-1509, 1121, HBC-19

WATER MANAGEMENT:
- Maintain 3-5 cm standing water during tillering
- Drain field for 3-4 days at panicle initiation (reduces sheath blight, blast)
- Final drainage: 15-20 days before harvest
- Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) method: saves 25-30% water without yield loss
