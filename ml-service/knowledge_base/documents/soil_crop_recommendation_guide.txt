COMPREHENSIVE SOIL HEALTH AND CROP RECOMMENDATION GUIDE — INDIA

SOURCE: ICAR-Indian Institute of Soil Science (IISS), Bhopal; Soil Health Mission, GoI
AUTHORITY: ICAR-NBSS&LUP (National Bureau of Soil Survey and Land Use Planning), Nagpur

=============================================================
SOIL pH AND CROP SUITABILITY
=============================================================

STRONGLY ACID (pH 4.5-5.5):
- Suitable crops: Tea, Coffee, Pineapple, Rubber
- Problems: Aluminum and Manganese toxicity; Phosphorus fixation
- Correction: Apply agricultural lime (CaCO3) at 2-4 tonnes/acre to raise pH

MODERATELY ACID (pH 5.5-6.5):
- Suitable crops: Rice, Maize, Sorghum, Finger Millet (Ragi), Groundnut, Cotton
- Ideal for most tropical crops
- Correction: 1-2 tonnes/acre lime if below 5.5

NEUTRAL (pH 6.5-7.5) — IDEAL:
- Most crops thrive: Wheat, Tomato, Potato, Vegetables, Sugarcane
- Maximum nutrient availability
- Maintenance: Organic matter addition (FYM, compost)

MODERATELY ALKALINE (pH 7.5-8.5):
- Suitable crops: Wheat, Barley, Sugar beet, Mustard
- Problems: Micronutrient deficiency (Iron, Zinc, Manganese)
- Correction: Apply sulfur 200 kg/acre, gypsum 400 kg/acre

STRONGLY ALKALINE (pH above 8.5) — SODIC/USAR:
- Very few crops tolerate: Barley, Rhodes grass
- Reclamation: Gypsum 2-4 tonnes/acre + leaching

=============================================================
SOIL TYPE CROP SUITABILITY — INDIA
=============================================================

RED LATERITE SOIL (Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Odisha):
- Characteristics: Low in nitrogen, phosphorus, organic matter; high iron/aluminum
- pH: Acidic (5.5-6.5)
- Best crops: Groundnut, Finger Millet (Ragi), Maize, Cotton, Castor, Sunflower, Coffee, Cashew
- Management: Apply FYM 5 tonnes/acre annually; lime if pH below 5.5; zinc and boron supplementation

BLACK COTTON SOIL / VERTISOL (Maharashtra, Karnataka northern, Telangana, Madhya Pradesh):
- Characteristics: High clay content, shrinks in dry, swells in wet; high pH 7.5-8.5
- Rich in calcium and magnesium; poor in nitrogen and phosphorus
- Best crops: Cotton, Sorghum (Jowar), Chickpea (Gram), Wheat (Rabi), Soybean
- Management: Deep plowing before monsoon; gypsum for structure; adequate drainage

ALLUVIAL SOIL (Gangetic Plain — UP, Bihar, Punjab, Haryana, West Bengal):
- Characteristics: Moderate to high fertility; pH neutral; good water retention
- Best crops: Wheat, Rice, Sugarcane, Maize, Potato, Vegetables
- Most productive agricultural soils in India
- Management: Balanced NPK; organic matter addition; micronutrient monitoring

SANDY LOAM SOIL (Rajasthan, Haryana):
- Characteristics: Low water retention; quick draining; low organic matter
- Best crops: Bajra (Pearl Millet), Moth Bean, Cluster Bean (Guar), Sesame, Groundnut
- Management: Drip irrigation; high organic matter; frequent light irrigation

COASTAL ALLUVIAL SOIL (Kerala, Konkan, Coastal Karnataka):
- Characteristics: High water table; often acidic; saline in coastal areas
- Best crops: Coconut, Arecanut, Banana, Rice, Vegetables
- Management: Raised bed cultivation; salinity management; good drainage

=============================================================
NITROGEN (N) DEFICIENCY AND MANAGEMENT
=============================================================

SYMPTOMS OF NITROGEN DEFICIENCY:
- General yellowing (chlorosis) starting from older lower leaves
- Plants stunted with thin, pale stems
- Premature leaf drop
- Reduced tillering in cereals

NITROGEN SOURCES AND APPLICATION:
- Urea (46% N): Most common; apply in 2-3 splits to reduce losses
- DAP (18% N + 46% P): Use as basal fertilizer
- Ammonium Sulfate (21% N): Good for alkaline soils
- CAN (26% N): Good for acidic soils

APPLICATION TIPS:
- Never apply urea in standing water in rice (denitrification loss)
- Apply urea when soil moisture is adequate; not before rain
- Neem-coated urea: 10-15% more efficient due to slow release
- Organic sources: FYM 10 tonnes/acre (provides N + P + K + micronutrients)

=============================================================
PHOSPHORUS (P) DEFICIENCY AND MANAGEMENT
=============================================================

SYMPTOMS:
- Purplish-red discoloration of leaves and stems
- Dark green leaves that turn bronze/purple
- Delayed maturity and poor root development
- Poor grain set in cereals

PHOSPHORUS SOURCES:
- DAP (46% P2O5): Best P source; apply at basal
- SSP (16% P2O5): Also contains calcium and sulfur; good for acidic soils
- Rock Phosphate: For acidic soils only; slow release
- Bone Meal: Organic source

APPLICATION TIPS:
- Apply phosphorus as basal (all at planting) — it doesn't move in soil
- Band placement more efficient than broadcast
- Avoid applying in alkaline soils with high calcium (P fixation)
- Mycorrhiza inoculants help in P uptake

=============================================================
POTASSIUM (K) DEFICIENCY AND MANAGEMENT
=============================================================

SYMPTOMS:
- Scorching (burning) of leaf margins, starting with older leaves
- Weak stems — plants lodge easily
- Poor quality fruits with reduced sugar content
- Reduced disease resistance

POTASSIUM SOURCES:
- MOP/KCl (60% K2O): Most common; cheap; avoid for chloride-sensitive crops
- SOP (50% K2O): Better for potato, tomato, grapes (no chloride)
- K2SO4: Good for sulfur-requiring crops

=============================================================
MICRONUTRIENT DEFICIENCIES
=============================================================

ZINC DEFICIENCY (Most Common in India):
- Symptoms: Interveinal chlorosis; white/buff streaks on young leaves; stunted growth
- Crops affected: Rice, Wheat, Maize, Cotton
- Correction: Zinc Sulfate (ZnSO4) 21%: 10 kg/acre as basal OR 0.5% foliar spray

BORON DEFICIENCY:
- Symptoms: Hollow stem in crucifers, empty pods in groundnut, fruit cracking
- Correction: Borax 10 kg/acre (basal) OR 0.3% Solubor foliar spray

IRON DEFICIENCY (Alkaline soils):
- Symptoms: Interveinal chlorosis on young leaves; veins remain green
- Correction: FeSO4 0.5% foliar spray (3-4 applications); acidify soil

=============================================================
ORGANIC FARMING AND SOIL HEALTH
=============================================================

FARMYARD MANURE (FYM):
- Well-decomposed FYM: Apply 10 tonnes/acre annually
- NPK content: approximately N=0.5%, P=0.25%, K=0.5%
- Benefits: Improves soil structure, water holding, microbial activity

VERMICOMPOST:
- Apply 2-4 tonnes/acre
- NPK content: N=1.5-2.5%, P=1.5-2%, K=1.5-2%
- Use for high-value vegetables and horticulture

BIOFERTILIZERS:
- Rhizobium: For pulse crops (seed treatment 200 g/10 kg seed)
- Azotobacter: For cereals and vegetables (seed treatment 200 g/10 kg)
- PSB (Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria): 200 g/10 kg seed
- Trichoderma: 2.5 kg/acre soil application (biocontrol + growth promotion)

GREEN MANURING:
- Dhaincha (Sesbania): 45-60 kg/acre; plow down before flowering (45-50 days)
- Sunn Hemp (Crotalaria): Adds 60-80 kg N/acre; 30 kg seed/acre
- Cow pea: 30 kg seed/acre; green manure at 45 days

SOIL CARBON IMPROVEMENT:
- Biochar: 1-2 tonnes/acre (long-term carbon sequestration)
- Cover crops in fallow period
- Avoid deep tillage (tractor plow) — use minimum tillage
